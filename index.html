Publishing a website (Guide)

Is your website ready and you want to put it on the internet? Here's how it works step by step for beginners:

  1. Choose hosting & domain: Buy a domain and a web hosting package from a German provider (e.g., Hetzner, Strato, Netcup).
  2. Open file manager: Log into your host's customer account and open the file manager (or connect via FTP).
  3. Upload files: Navigate to the public folder (usually public_html or html) and upload your index.html.
  4. Activate SSL: Activate a free SSL certificate (Let's Encrypt) in the hosting area so your website is securely accessible via HTTPS.
